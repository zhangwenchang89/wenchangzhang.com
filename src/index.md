::: bio
I am an Assistant Professor at the Kelley School of Business, Indiana University Bloomington.

**My research focuses on platform operations and online market design, with the goal of understanding how digital platforms reduce market frictions and improve market performance. I study how digital platforms address core operational challenges—including participation, matching, market expansion, and information frictions—and examine the broader economic and social implications of platform-based markets.**

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Management from the Robert H. Smith School of Business at the University of Maryland, co-advised by [Wedad Elmaghraby](https://www.rhsmith.umd.edu/directory/wedad-j-elmaghraby) and [Kostas Bimpikis](http://stanford.edu/~kostasb/) (Stanford).
:::
